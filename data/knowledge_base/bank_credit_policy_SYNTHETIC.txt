SYNTHETIC INTERNAL CREDIT POLICY — Bharat Trust Bank (FICTITIOUS)
Internal reference document — Retail Lending Division
(This bank, and this policy, are entirely fictitious — written for a
capstone project demo. Any resemblance to a real institution's policy
is coincidental.)

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 Minimum age: 21 years at the time of application, maximum age at
    loan maturity: 65 years for salaried applicants, 70 years for
    self-employed applicants with demonstrated business continuity.
1.2 Minimum monthly income: ₹5,000 (verified or declared). Applicants
    below this floor are routed to manual review rather than auto-
    declined, since a co-applicant or alternative income evidence can
    qualify them.
1.3 Minimum employment/business tenure: 1 year in current job (salaried)
    or 2 years of business continuity (self-employed).

2. LOAN AMOUNT AND TENURE

2.1 Personal loans: ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000, tenure 6 to 60 months.
2.2 The requested amount is assessed against the applicant's income and
    existing obligations as part of the risk model; there is no fixed
    income-multiple cap in this platform's automated rules.

3. DEBT-TO-INCOME (DTI) POLICY

3.1 The platform computes a debt ratio = existing monthly debt payments
    (EMIs) divided by monthly income. A debt ratio ABOVE 100% (i.e.
    obligations exceed income) is declined automatically by hard rule,
    regardless of credit score.
3.2 A debt ratio below 100% is not an automatic decline: it is one of the
    inputs to the credit risk model, where a higher ratio (and especially
    one above roughly 50-55%) increases the predicted default probability
    and so makes MANUAL_REVIEW or DECLINE more likely.

4. DELINQUENCY HISTORY POLICY

4.1 Three or more instances of 90+ day delinquency in the last 24 months
    results in automatic decline, regardless of current income or DTI.
4.2 One or two instances of 90+ day delinquency are NOT an automatic
    decline; they are a strong risk input to the credit risk model and
    will usually push the predicted default probability up.
4.3 Any history of 30-59 or 60-89 day delinquency alone (without a 90+
    day instance) is treated as a risk-scoring input rather than a hard
    rule trigger.

5. DEPENDENTS AND HOUSEHOLD SIZE

5.1 Applications from households with 6 or more dependents are routed to
    manual review to assess per-capita disposable income more closely,
    rather than relying on gross household income alone.

6. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS

6.1 All applicants: government-issued photo ID, address proof.
6.2 Salaried applicants: last 3 months' salary slips, last 6 months'
    bank statement showing salary credits.
6.3 Self-employed applicants: last 2 years' ITR, last 12 months' bank
    statement, business continuity proof (GST registration or
    equivalent).

7. PRICING

7.1 Base rate is set monthly by the Treasury desk. Final rate = base
    rate + risk premium, where the risk premium is determined by the
    applicant's model-predicted default probability band (LOW/MEDIUM/
    HIGH as produced by Module 1 of the underwriting platform).

8. REVIEW AND ESCALATION

8.1 Any MANUAL_REVIEW decision must be actioned by a human underwriter
    within 2 business days.
8.2 An applicant may request the specific reason for a DECLINE decision;
    the underwriting platform's Decision Engine (Module 4) logs a
    structured, human-readable explanation for every decision to support
    this.
